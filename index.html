All 87 meetings — full data table with sources
Dissent counts, chair, and vote totals are drawn from the Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis's "A History of FOMC Dissents" tracking file (fomc_dissents_data.xlsx, last updated 7/31/2026), which already classifies each dissent as tighter/easier/other relative to the action taken. Target-range levels and action sizes are derived from FRED series DFEDTARU/DFEDTARL, matched to each meeting date. Vote-line wording and named preferences are quoted from the relevant federalreserve.gov statement, linked per row in the table above and in the hover readout.
No chart library, no framework, no build step — hand-rolled SVG and vanilla JS reading a single embedded dataset.
